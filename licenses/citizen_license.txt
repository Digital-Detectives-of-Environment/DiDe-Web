1. WHAT YOU DOWNLOADED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Environmental reports made by volunteers on the DiDe platform:
  * only ACTIVE reports of PUBLIC event types;
  * fields: event_id, latitude, longitude, event_type, description,
    created_at, photo_urls, num_agrees, direction;
  * the photos of these reports (folder "photos/").
No account data is included (no names, usernames, IDs or roles).
If the platform uses an aggregation layer (a grid of areas), you do NOT get
individual reports: the archive contains "grid_events.geojson", one feature
per grid cell (the cell polygon) with its key column(s), the number of
reports (event_count) and the number per event type (counts_by_type).
No coordinates, descriptions or photos are included in that case.
The data is crowd-sourced and has NOT been verified.
